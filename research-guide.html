Research Guide to the Sunagawa Struggle and Sunagawa Case

An English entry point to the archive, for graduate students and researchers in Japanese history, Cold War history, and the U.S.–Japan alliance / 英語圏の研究者・学生のためのアーカイブ入門（日本語版はこちら）

What this archive is

The Sunagawa Heiwa Hiroba Public Article Archive (砂川平和ひろば公開記事アーカイブ) is a static preservation site. It holds 208 public articles published by the community organization Sunagawa Heiwa Hiroba on two former platforms: the FC2 website (90 articles) and the Ameblo blog (118 articles). Each preserved article keeps a link to its original page, so the chain from original publication to this copy is traceable.

All 208 preserved articles remain readable, with full-text search and filters by year, language, and content type. The archive is not the organization's current homepage, and it is not an official record of any court.

Scope

  • Captured on 2026-09-05 from FC2 and Ameblo
  • Languages: Japanese 190, English 10, Japanese + English 5, Chinese 3
  • Coverage: the Sunagawa Struggle (1955–1956), the Sunagawa Incident (1957) and the 1959 Date Judgment, the postwar anti-base movement, the civil damages lawsuit (国賠訴訟, filed 2019, Tokyo High Court judgment 2025-01-31), and the ひろば's current local activity

Suggested reading path

For the topic itself, three short editorial pages are available in Japanese (and are internally linked to the preserved sources):

  • 砂川事件とは — what the Sunagawa Incident was, and how it led to the 1959 Date Judgment
  • 伊達判決とは — the 1959 district court judgment, its reversal by the Supreme Court, and the civil damages case
  • 砂川闘争年表 — a chronology from 1609 to the return of Tachikawa Air Base in 1977, linked to preserved articles
  • 国賠訴訟年表 — the civil damages lawsuit through 2025-01-31

A bilingual reading path is the 砂川闘争入門 / Guide to the Sunagawa Struggle.

Provenance and citation

Each preserved article shows its original platform, publication date, and an “Original page / 元ページ” link, plus a citation block with Copy archive URL and Copy citation buttons. Suggested citation form:

Article title. Original publication date. 砂川平和ひろば公開記事アーカイブ, archive URL. Original URL (FC2 or Ameblo).

Rights to original text and images remain with the original authors and rights holders. This site is a public-web snapshot, not an official record.
